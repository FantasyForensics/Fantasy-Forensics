Fantasy Football Trade Calculator

Grade any fantasy football trade in seconds — dynasty or redraft, priced in your league's scoring. Add players and draft picks to each side and get a fair verdict, not a vibe.

How it works

1. Connect your league — or don't. Connect a Sleeper or ESPN league and every grade is priced in your league's real scoring and settings. As a guest you can still grade any trade against the full player pool.

2. Add players and picks to each side. Search any player or draft pick. Picks are priced on the same scale as players, so a future first reads as the veteran it would take to buy it.

3. Read the verdict. Both sides get a total and the trade grades Fair, Close, or Lopsided — plus what would balance the deal if it's off. Totals are adjusted for trade shape, so a two-for-one doesn't get full credit for both players: the gap you see is the gap that matters.

What makes a trade fair?

A fair trade isn't two equal sums — it's two sides you'd be happy to sit on. The calculator prices every asset on one scale, adjusts for how concentrated each side is, and grades the result. A 1-for-1 swap grades on the raw values; anything else gets the shape adjustment. If the verdict says lopsided, the balancer shows the pick or player that evens it.
