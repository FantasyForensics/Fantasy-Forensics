Fantasy Football Power Rankings

Who is actually most likely to win your league — and what is every team worth as a franchise? Two rankings, two different questions, one page each.

Current Power — who wins the title?

Current Power is the chance each team wins the championship, from thousands of simulations of the rest of the season: playoff odds, rest-of-season projections and the real remaining schedule, all baked in. A team with no real playoff path can't rank high — its title chance is capped by its playoff odds.

Franchise Value — what is the team worth?

Franchise Value is the roster plus the picks, players and draft picks shown as separate subtotals. It answers the long question: how much is this team worth as an asset, not just this season.

Trade stance — buy, hold, or sell?

Every team also carries a stance from its playoff odds: Buyer, Holding, Seller, or Maximal Seller. The stance drives the trade tools — who to target, who is selling, and what a firesale partner looks like. A team can top one ranking and sit mid-table on the other, and that's the point: strength now and value later are different things.

How to use the rankings

Check your Current Power rank to see where you really stand, then check your stance before you offer: buyers chase win-now pieces, sellers collect picks, and the teams in the middle get two pivot paths. The full rankings live in the app, per league, with every team graded against its own league.
