Falling prices aren't all buy windows

A discount is only a discount when the fall is about price rather than role. Jared Goff at 5,654 still carries a top price — the 161-point drop is real, but so is the context underneath it, which is why the board doesn't call him cheap yet.

What studs actually cost

At the top of the board, the week's moves were small — and the completed-trade record shows what the top tier actually fetches. Our Real Trades database — 2,582 completed deals from public leagues — has its most recent top-tier deal on October 18, when Bhayshul Tuten, Trevor Lawrence, Chris Olave and Jaylen Warren were sent for Drake Maye (8,567 on today's board), Omarion Hampton (3,912 on today's board), a 2027 1st, a 2027 2nd and a 2028 1st. Packages, not discounts. Jahmyr Gibbs still holds 9,489 (RB1); he has changed hands 19 times in the database, most recently on September 17, when he returned Caleb Douglas (3,058 on today's board), Puka Nacua (7,783 on today's board) and a 2028 2nd.

The lesson of the week cuts one way: prices move every week, and an offer priced on last week's board is already stale.
